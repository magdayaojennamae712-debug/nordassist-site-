    Our Story

    Why NordAssist exists.

    Most founders don't have a time problem. They have a priority problem. The day quietly fills up with inbox triage, scheduling, follow-ups, expense reports, travel logistics, and admin tasks that don't fit anyone's job description.

    Hiring locally in Finland or the Nordics is expensive (€4–6k+ per month, fully loaded), slow, and usually over-qualified for the actual work. So the work stays on your plate.

    "We don't sell hours. We sell the part of your week back where you stop apologising for not getting to things."

    NordAssist gives you a single, dedicated executive assistant who works with you week after week — not a rotating pool, not a chatbot, not a task marketplace. They learn your tools, your preferences, and your context, and the relationship gets stronger over time.

    Every assistant is fluent in English, college-educated, trained in executive support, and based in the Philippines on hours that overlap with the European workday. We handle hiring, onboarding, supervision, performance, and backup coverage.

    You get back ten to twenty hours a week. We make sure the work gets done well.

    Trust & Security

    We work with your inbox. We take that seriously.

    An executive assistant only works if the trust holds. Every NordAssist team member is vetted, contracted under NDA, and trained on confidentiality and data handling before their first day with you.

    GDPR-aligned

    Data handling practices designed around EU privacy standards. Documented, reviewable, and updated annually.

    Secure by default

    1Password-based credential sharing, MFA required, company-managed equipment, and least-privilege system access.

    Continuity guaranteed

    If your assistant is sick or on leave, a trained backup steps in. Access is revoked the moment an engagement ends.

    A Note from the Founder

    Why I built NordAssist.

    Jennamae Saarela
    Founder & CEO · NordAssist
    📍 Oulu, Finland  ·  🇵🇭 Originally from the Philippines
    Most founders I know across Finland and the Nordics are doing two jobs: the one they hired themselves for, and the operational work that nobody hired them to do. NordAssist exists to close that gap.

    I was born and raised in the Philippines, where I saw firsthand how incredibly skilled, dedicated, and professional Filipino workers are. We grow up multilingual, college-educated, and trained to work hard for the people we serve. Filipino VAs don't just work for you — they work with you.

    When I moved to Finland, I fell in love with Nordic culture — the directness, the trust, the respect for work-life balance. But I also saw something that surprised me: brilliant founders and CEOs buried in emails, scheduling, admin, and tasks that were stealing hours from their real work every single week.

    Hiring locally in Finland costs €4,000–6,000 per month minimum. Most founders couldn't justify it. So they kept doing the work themselves. I knew there was a better way — because I came from it.

    In 2026 I launched NordAssist — a fully managed VA agency connecting Finnish and Nordic executives with highly trained Filipino Virtual Assistants. Not a freelance marketplace. A real, managed service where we handle everything — hiring, training, supervision, backup coverage — so you can just delegate and grow.

    I'm based in Oulu, Finland. My VAs are based in the Philippines. And together, we give Nordic founders their time back.
