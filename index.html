The problem

Your calendar is full of work only you can do — and a lot of work that anyone could.

Most founders don't have a time problem. They have a priority problem. The day quietly fills up with inbox triage, scheduling, follow-ups, expense reports, travel logistics, and admin tasks that don't fit anyone's job description.

Hiring locally in Finland or the Nordics is expensive (€4–6k+ per month, fully loaded), slow, and usually over-qualified for the actual work. So the work stays on your plate, and the strategic thinking gets pushed to evenings and weekends.

It's not sustainable, and it's not necessary.

The solution

One dedicated assistant. Trained, supervised, and matched to how you work.

NordAssist gives you a single, dedicated executive assistant who works with you week after week — not a rotating pool, not a chatbot, not a task marketplace. They learn your tools, your preferences, and your context, and the relationship gets stronger over time.

Every assistant is fluent in English, college-educated, trained in executive support, and based in the Philippines on hours that overlap with the European workday. We handle hiring, onboarding, supervision, performance, and backup coverage.

You get back ten to twenty hours a week. We make sure the work gets done well.

FAQ

Common questions, answered honestly.

How is this different from Upwork or Fiverr?

You're not managing a freelancer. With NordAssist you get a dedicated assistant we have hired, trained, and supervised — plus an account manager who steps in if anything is off. You delegate; we handle everything else.

Will my assistant's English be strong enough to handle client communication?

Yes. We screen for written and spoken English at a level appropriate for executive correspondence, including drafting on your behalf. The Philippines has one of the largest professional English-speaking workforces in the world, and we filter aggressively from there.

What about the time zone difference?

The Philippines is 5–6 hours ahead of Finland. Our assistants work shifts that overlap with the European workday, typically from late morning EU time onward. Some founders actually prefer it — work that goes in at end-of-day Helsinki is often done by morning.

How do you handle data privacy and GDPR?

NDAs are signed before any access is granted. Credentials are shared via 1Password with MFA. Access is scoped to what's needed and revoked at the end of an engagement. Our data handling practices are GDPR-aligned and documented for client review.

What if my assistant leaves or doesn't work out?

Two things. First, we cover absences with a trained backup so you're never left without support. Second, if the match isn't right, we replace the assistant — at our cost, not yours. Continuity is part of what you're paying for.

Can I try it before committing?

Yes. We offer a two-week paid trial at a reduced rate. If it's not the right fit by the end of the trial, you walk away with no further commitment.

Do you work with founders outside Finland?

Yes. Our focus markets are Finland, Sweden, Norway, and Estonia, and we also work with founders elsewhere in the EU. The only requirement is that work is conducted in English.
